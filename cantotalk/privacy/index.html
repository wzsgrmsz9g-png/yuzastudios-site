CantoTalk — Privacy Policy

CantoTalk is published by YuZa Studio, the studio name of Clay Cavanagh (the App Store seller).

Effective date: 17 October 2026 (adds In-app purchase)
Contact: support@yuzastudios.com

What CantoTalk is

CantoTalk helps you practise everyday Hong Kong Cantonese on iPhone and iPad. Lessons, listening drills and self-recording work offline, without an account. The only network activity is Apple’s App Store system, which handles the optional in-app purchase (see In-app purchase below).

Data on your device

  • Lesson completion, review progress and practice self-checks (UserDefaults)
  • Short-lived microphone recordings while you practise — stored only on this device and removed when you leave the practice screen or background the app (iOS may clean leftover temp files after an abrupt termination)
  • Whether the course unlock is active (a cached flag; Apple’s StoreKit remains the source of truth)

We do not collect personal data, create accounts, or upload progress or recordings. Purchases are handled by Apple (see In-app purchase).

Audio and microphone

Text-to-speech uses Apple’s on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. When you tap Record, the app uses AVAudioRecorder to capture audio locally so you can listen back. Recordings stay on the device and are not uploaded. Microphone permission is requested only when you record.

Network, SDKs and tracking

CantoTalk does not send your lessons, practice, progress, recordings or any other data to us or to any server we operate. To show prices and to process, restore and verify the optional in-app purchase, Apple’s App Store / StoreKit system on your device communicates with Apple’s servers. CantoTalk does not include analytics, advertising or other third-party SDKs. CantoTalk does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children

Not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Deny microphone access in iOS Settings
  • Delete the app to remove on-device progress and any leftover local recordings

In-app purchase

CantoTalk 1.1 offers an optional one-time purchase, the CantoTalk course unlock (US$14.99 or the local equivalent shown in the App Store). It is a non-consumable purchase: you buy it once and it stays unlocked for your Apple Account.

Apple processes the payment. Purchases are made through the App Store using Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Apple handles your payment method, billing details, receipts and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment information.

How your purchase is checked. CantoTalk uses Apple’s StoreKit to ask whether your Apple Account owns the unlock. Apple digitally signs this information, and the app checks it on your device. It also checks Apple’s signed record of when you first installed the app, so people who installed before version 1.1 keep full access. The app saves a small “unlocked” flag on your device so it opens quickly; this flag is not sent anywhere. “Restore Purchases” asks Apple to re-sync your purchases.

What goes over the network. To show the price, complete a purchase, restore it or confirm it, iOS’s App Store / StoreKit system communicates with Apple’s servers. That communication is between your device and Apple. CantoTalk does not send purchase, payment or any other data to us or to any server we operate, and it contains no third-party purchase, analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not receive a list of who has bought the unlock, apart from the aggregated sales reports Apple gives all developers. Those reports don’t identify you.

Family Sharing. The course unlock is not shared through Family Sharing.

Refunds. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds the purchase, the unlock is removed the next time the app checks with StoreKit.
