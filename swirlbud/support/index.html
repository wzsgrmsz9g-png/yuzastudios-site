Swirlbud — Support

Contact Yuza Studios

For help with Swirlbud, email support@yuzastudios.com.

Please tell us your device model and iOS version, and don't send photos of your child.

Privacy questions

You can use the same address for questions about Swirlbud privacy.

What's free, and Unlock Swirlbud

  • Free, always: every sample page, your first 3 photos, every finished page and the Album.
  • "Unlock Swirlbud" is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It lets you turn as many of your own photos into pages as you like.
  • The price is shown in the app before you buy.
  • You buy it from behind the parent check in the app.

Restoring your purchase

If you bought Unlock Swirlbud and it isn't showing, open the Parent area (it's behind the parent check) and tap "Restore purchase". You can also restore from the Unlock screen. Use the Apple Account you bought with. If your family uses Family Sharing, other family members may be able to use the purchase and can restore it with their own Apple Account.

Refunds and purchase problems

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we can't look up or change your purchase. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose "Request a refund". For other problems with a payment, contact Apple.

The parent check

Some things in Swirlbud need a grown-up: buying, restoring, sharing, saving to Photos, settings, deleting, and links out of the app. To do these, you hold a button for 3 seconds and then answer a simple sum. It keeps those actions away from young children; it isn't a lock or a way of proving age.

Camera or photos not working

Swirlbud asks permission before it uses the camera or your photo library. If you said no, you can turn access back on: open the Settings app and find Swirlbud.

Deleting pictures

A parent can delete a book, a picture or an album page, after the parent check and a confirmation. Deleting does not give back one of your first 3 photos. To remove everything Swirlbud keeps on your device, delete the app. Pictures you already saved to Photos or shared are not removed.
