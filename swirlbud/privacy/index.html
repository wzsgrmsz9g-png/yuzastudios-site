Swirlbud Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Swirlbud is a colouring app for young children and their parents, made by Yuza Studios. This policy explains what happens to the photos and other information you use in Swirlbud.

The short version

  • Swirlbud has no account and no sign-in.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking or advertising code.
  • The app itself does not send your photos, your child's pictures or any other information to Yuza Studios or to any third party.
  • What you make in Swirlbud is kept in the app on your device.

Photos and pages

  • You can take a photo with the camera or pick photos from your photo library. Swirlbud turns each one into a colouring page on your device.
  • Swirlbud asks iOS for permission before it uses the camera or your photo library. You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.
  • Swirlbud keeps its own copy of each photo you use, plus the pages, colouring progress and finished pictures, inside the app on your device. Swirlbud does not change or delete the original photo in your photo library.

Other information kept on your device

  • If a parent adds a child's first name in the Parent area, Swirlbud keeps it on the device and shows it on book covers. It is up to 12 characters, and a parent can change or remove it there.
  • Swirlbud also keeps settings (such as sound and music), tutorial progress, and a few simple counts it uses to decide when to ask whether you'd like to rate the app.
  • None of this is sent to us.

Purchases

Swirlbud has one optional in-app purchase, "Unlock Swirlbud". It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it can only be started from behind the parent check. Apple handles the purchase, the payment and any refund through your Apple Account. Yuza Studios does not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account details, and Swirlbud does not send any information about the purchase to us or to any third party. To know whether Swirlbud is unlocked, and to let you restore the purchase, Swirlbud checks with Apple's purchase system on your device.

Swirlbud also counts how many of your first 3 photos have been used. That count is kept only on your device and is not sent to us.

When a picture leaves the app

A picture leaves Swirlbud only when a parent chooses to send it out. Both of these actions sit behind the parent check:

  • Save to Photos: Swirlbud saves a finished picture to your photo library, and asks iOS for permission the first time.
  • Share: Swirlbud opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where the picture goes is your choice, and what happens to it then is up to the app or person you send it to.

Apple services

Some things are handled by Apple, not by us, under Apple's own privacy policy:

  • your photo library and camera;
  • in-app purchases;
  • the App Store rating prompt;
  • device backups.

Apple may give us crash reports from people who have chosen in their device settings to share them with app developers.

Backups

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or a computer, iOS may include Swirlbud's data in that backup. You control those backups.

Deleting things

  • Deleting a book, a picture or an album page sits behind the parent check and asks you to confirm.
  • To remove everything Swirlbud keeps on your device, delete the app.
  • Deleting inside Swirlbud does not remove pictures you already saved to Photos or shared, or copies in your device backups.
  • Deleting a photo does not give back one of your first 3 photos.

Children's privacy

Swirlbud is made for children aged 5 and under to use with a parent. We do not collect personal information from children through the app. Swirlbud has a parent check, a grown-up task that keeps purchases, sharing, saving, settings, deleting and links out of the app away from young children. The parent check is not a way of proving age. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Contacting us

For questions about Swirlbud privacy, contact Yuza Studios at support@yuzastudios.com.

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address and your message. We use it only to reply to you. We keep it only as long as we need it, and you can ask us to delete it. Please don't send photos of your child.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We add no analytics, advertising or tracking to this page.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
