Receiptly — Privacy Policy

Receiptly is published by YuZa Studio.

Effective date: 30 October 2026
Contact: support@yuzastudios.com

The short version

  • Receiptly reads your receipts on your iPhone. Your receipts, photos and scanned text stay on your device.
  • We do not collect any data. Receiptly has no account, no sign-in and no bank linking, and we run no server for it.
  • Receiptly has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you.
  • Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What Receiptly is

Receiptly is an iPhone app for saving and organising receipts. You can add a receipt in several ways:

  • take a photo, or scan a document with the camera
  • pick photos
  • open a file (PDF, image or email)
  • paste text
  • share a receipt to Receiptly from another app

Receiptly reads the merchant, date, total and other details, and keeps everything on your device.

How your receipts are read: on your device

  • Receiptly reads text with Apple’s Vision framework, Apple’s document scanner (VisionKit) and PDFKit for PDFs. All of these run on your iPhone.
  • On iOS 26 and later, where Apple’s on-device model is available, Receiptly may also use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models to help pick out receipt details. This model also runs on your iPhone.
  • Receiptly does not send your receipts to us, to Apple or to anyone else to be read.
  • Receiptly makes no network requests of its own. Reading receipts doesn’t need an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Receiptly stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage:

  • Saved receipts: the details Receiptly read (such as merchant, date, amounts and category), the receipt image or images, and the receipt text.
  • Learned categories: when you change a receipt’s category, Receiptly remembers that choice for that merchant so it can suggest it next time.
  • Usage counter: how many scans you’ve used this month on the free plan.
  • Subscription status: this is read from Apple’s StoreKit on your device (see Subscriptions).

Receiptly doesn’t use iCloud, CloudKit or any other sync, and nothing is sent to any server of ours. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or a computer, iOS may include Receiptly’s data in that backup. You control those backups.

Sharing receipts into Receiptly (share extension)

You can share a receipt to Receiptly from other apps, for example Mail, Files, Photos or Safari. The extension accepts PDFs, images, emails, plain text and HTML. You can share up to 20 items at a time.

  • The extension reads what you share on your device. It passes it to the Receiptly app through a private shared folder on your iPhone that only Receiptly and its extension can use.
  • After Receiptly imports the receipt, it deletes that hand-off copy. If an import fails, or you’ve reached your monthly scan limit, the copy stays in the folder so Receiptly can try again later. The copy is also deleted when you delete the receipt.
  • Receiptly does not open web links. If you share a web address, Receiptly does not download it. It asks you to share the receipt itself, its PDF or a screenshot.
  • Emails and documents can include personal details, such as your name, address or the last digits of a card number. These details stay on your device with the saved receipt.

Camera, photos and clipboard

  • Camera: Receiptly asks for camera access only so you can photograph or scan receipts.
  • Photos: Receiptly reads only the photos you pick.
  • You can turn off either permission at any time in iOS Settings › Receiptly. You can still add receipts in other ways.
  • Receiptly reads the clipboard only when you choose Paste.

Exports

You can export receipts as a CSV file or a PDF. Exports are created on your device and go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet. Once you share an export, whoever receives it handles it under their own terms. Receiptly deletes its temporary export files when you delete receipts.

Subscriptions (Receiptly Pro)

Receiptly is free to use with a monthly scan limit. Receiptly Pro is an optional, auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s App Store in-app purchase system (StoreKit). It comes in two options:

  • Monthly, renewing every month. Apple may offer a reduced price for the first month, or a one-week free trial on some offers.
  • Annual, renewing every year.

The price is shown in the app and on the App Store, in your local currency, before you buy.

  • Apple processes the payment. Apple handles your payment method, billing, receipts, renewals and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment information.
  • Renewal and cancellation. A subscription renews automatically, and your Apple Account is charged, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or in your Apple Account settings in the App Store. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. If you cancel during a free trial, you won’t be charged.
  • How your subscription is checked. Receiptly asks Apple’s StoreKit on your device whether your Apple Account has an active subscription. Apple signs this information, and Receiptly checks it on your device. We run no server that checks purchases.
  • Restore Purchases asks Apple to re-sync your purchases.
  • What goes over the network. To show prices, and to complete, restore or check a purchase, iOS’s App Store system talks to Apple’s servers. That connection is between your device and Apple. We only receive the aggregated sales reports Apple gives all developers, and these don’t identify you.
  • Family Sharing. Receiptly Pro subscriptions are not shared through Family Sharing.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apple’s standard Terms of Use (EULA) apply: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Network, SDKs and tracking

  • Receiptly sends no receipts, images, text, categories, usage or other data to us or to any server we operate.
  • Receiptly includes no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • Receiptly does not track you across apps or websites, and it doesn’t use the advertising identifier.
  • If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us crash and usage statistics. Apple controls this, and the statistics don’t identify you to us.

Deleting your data

Your data is on your iPhone, so you control it:

  • Delete receipts in the app. This removes the saved receipt, its images and any hand-off copy.
  • Settings › Delete all receipts removes every saved receipt and image, the learned categories, and Receiptly’s temporary export files from this iPhone. This can’t be undone.
  • Settings › Forget learned categories clears only the learned categories.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Deleting receipts doesn’t reset your monthly free-scan count, which also stays on your device.

Children

Receiptly is designed for adults keeping track of their purchases. It is not directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, California’s CCPA/CPRA, Canada’s PIPEDA and Hong Kong’s PDPO may give you rights over personal data a business holds about you.

Receiptly sends no personal data to us, so we hold none about you to access, correct, delete or transfer. You can see and delete everything on your device using the steps above. We do not sell or share personal information.

If you have a privacy question, email support@yuzastudios.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Receiptly handles data, we’ll update this policy and its effective date before the change ships, and update the App Store privacy label.
